3. "From C to Q": The buzz term in quantum computing is the quantum analog of a `bit` which is the "qubit" or "quantum bit". A regular bit is a binary digit 0 or 1 on a classical computer. A qubit we'll get to in a moment. Just to say: The real power of quantum computing comes from having multiple qubits available to work with... but a single qubit is conceptually challenging all by itself. In the spirit of "baby steps" the quantum computing part of this presentation sticks to just a single qubit.

    What I'm going to describe is a two-stage process applied to a quantum system (of one qubit). This is very suspicious. In the first stage: Something impossible happens: The qubit is "two things at once" but only because we have not measured it. In the second stage: We measure the qubit and poof it proves to be either one thing or another but not both. Straight out of Alice in Wonderland.
